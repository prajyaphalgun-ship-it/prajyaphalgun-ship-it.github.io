PaymentIncidentAI

Payment Incident Monitoring & Investigation Prototype

Designed and deployed a Python-based prototype to demonstrate how payment operations teams can monitor transaction anomalies, identify potential incidents and prioritize investigations using structured operational data.

Business Problem

Payment failures across ATM, POS and issuer-host environments can affect transaction success rates and create operational disruption. This prototype demonstrates a structured approach to identifying unusual transaction patterns and supporting faster incident assessment.

Key Capabilities

  • Monitors synthetic transaction data across ATM withdrawal timeouts, POS payment declines and issuer-host failures.
  • Uses configurable thresholds to identify unusual transaction patterns.
  • Applies P1–P4 incident prioritization to support operational triage.
  • Generates scenario-based investigation recommendations for payment support teams.
  • Estimates transaction value affected to help assess potential business impact.
  • Generates downloadable incident reports for operational review.
